## ⚠️ Important: how these may and may not be used

The people in the stock photographs are **not DBU patients**. They are used atmospherically
only — to carry the mood of the jaw, the profile and the clinical setting.

They must never be captioned, positioned or implied as:

- patients of the clinic
- treatment results, before/afters, or testimonials
- members of Dr Uy's team

Alt text throughout the site is written to describe the image neutrally for this reason. If
Dr Uy supplies real clinic photography, portraits or consented patient images, these should
be replaced — her own always beats stock.
